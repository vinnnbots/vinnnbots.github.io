Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

VinnnBots is a private, single-user tool used by its owner to access the owner's own Google account data through Google APIs.

Data accessed

The application requests access to the Google services the owner explicitly grants during OAuth consent. Access is limited to those granted scopes.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform actions requested by the owner. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own server. Google user data is not stored on external servers operated by this application.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
